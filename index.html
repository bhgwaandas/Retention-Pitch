Global D2C wellness brand · RFM & cohort segmentation, margin strategy

Turned a written-off premium category into a top-3 seller

Problem
The brand's most premium category (priced 70–100% above its standard range) was treated as slow-moving stock and liquidated at ~50% off every season to clear inventory — destroying margin on its highest-value products.
Action
Rebuilt the category's go-to-market around RFM and cohort segmentation across US and global stores — matching the right customers to the right message instead of blanket discounting, shifting from liquidation to targeted full-price sell-through.
Result
Doubled contribution margin (CM1 ~25% → ~50%), held average discount to just ~10%, cleared inventory ahead of season, and made it a top-3 category by revenue two years running.
Global D2C wellness brand · Segmentation, lifecycle optimisation

Made an existing email list worth twice as much

Problem
Email was a major revenue channel but underperforming — broad, untargeted sends meant low revenue per recipient and wasted send volume and cost.
Action
Re-architected audience segmentation using RFM, purchase, and engagement signals so every send matched customer intent, and sunset unengaged profiles to sharpen targeting and cut waste.
Result
More than doubled revenue per recipient ($0.07 → $0.15+) — growing email revenue while lowering send costs. More money from the same list.
Global D2C wellness brand · Lifecycle strategy, email/SMS/push, 0-to-1 build

Built a multi-channel retention engine from zero

Problem
The brand was launching a new product line with no retention infrastructure — no flows, no lifecycle, no repeat-purchase engine for the new range.
Action
Built the retention engine from scratch across email, SMS, and push — designing and launching lifecycle flows (welcome, education, replenishment, winback) and campaigns, then iterating through experimentation.
Result
Stood up a full multi-channel retention program from zero that supported a seven-figure projected ARR for the new line within its first year.
Global D2C wellness brand · Email deliverability (specialist)

Recovered email revenue hidden by poor inbox placement

Problem
Email revenue was capped by poor deliverability — campaigns and flows were landing in spam and promotions tabs, throttling a channel that should have been performing.
Action
Diagnosed and re-architected deliverability at the inbox-provider level — authentication, sender reputation, list hygiene, and provider-specific optimisation — to move mail back into the primary inbox.
Result
Doubled inbox placement and open rates (~25% → ~50%) and lifted email revenue 50% — unlocking revenue already sitting in the list.
